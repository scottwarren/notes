RSU rules as I understand them

1. If shares are sold within 30 days of acquiring, then tax is paid on the sell value
2. If shares are sold after 30 days, but within 12 months, capital gains tax is due
3. If shares are sold after 12 months, you are eligible for a 50% discount on the capital gains tax.
4. The value of the shares at the vesting date is applied to Income tax for the year that they vest. That is except if sold within 30 days, where the value at the sale date which gets added onto your income.
5.  Regardless of when you sell them, they are still considered income tax, so you need to withhold your top tax bracket's tax rate + 2% for the medicare levy
