
_Really? Potatoes?/Potato allergy, really?_

Yes.

_I've never heard of anyone that's allergic to potatoes/OMG, never heard of such allergy_

Yes, me neither.

_Do you want a chip/some fries/etc?_

No, I don't want any chips/fries, but thanks for asking :D

_Are you also allergic to <thing that is made from white potato>?_

Yes, anything that has white potato in it (hash browns, fries, potato chips, crisps, mashed potato, wedges, etc).

_Are you allergic to things that also contains white potato (e.g. most soups)?_

Yes.

_What about Vodka?/Are you allergic to Vodka?_

I am not allergic to [potato based Vodka](https://onthegas.org/drink/best-potato-vodka)

_What happens when you eat white potato?_

It's kind of hard to describe, but my saliva gets very thick, it's very hard to swallow, and my throat is irritated in a way that's kind of painful.

_Will you die if you eat white potato?_

No, I won't die if I eat potato.

_Have you always been allergic?_

No, I started having this reaction when I was about 19. At this time I was a poor lazy uni student, who ate at McDonalds a lot, and ate hash browns from there far too often (once more than 5), I believe the over-exposure to potato contributed to me developing the allergy.

_Are you okay?/I am so sorry!/Are you upset about it?/That must suck._

Yes I am fine, although at first it was hard to deal with it, since I really enjoyed potato based things, but I have gotten used to it. Sweet Potato makes a really great alternative to white potato, so when it's available, I choose that option, otherwise I just go without.

_Are you allergic to other vegetables from the same family (i.e. root vegetables)?_

No, although I am not sure why.

_Are you allergic to other types of potato?_

No, I regularly eat sweet potato, I'm not sure why I am allergic to only white potato

_Can you eat things if they have touched white potato?_

Yes, as long as they themselves don't contain white potato, I can eat it, regardless of if the piece of food touched white potato.

_Do you have a reaction if you touch white potato?_

No, I just can't digest it.

_Are you really allergic?_/_Are you still allergic?_

Yes, I am really allergic. I occasionally accidentally eat something that contains potato and get sick, so as far as I know, yes I am still allergic. I am not periodically making myself intentionally sick just to see on the off chance that I am no longer allergic.
